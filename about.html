About NZINC

Understanding places.
Enabling change.

We bring research and people together to support a more resilient relationship between communities and the natural world.

Who we are

A research-minded team with a community-first outlook.

We work across aquatic research, environmental understanding and sustainable development in Nepal.

Our approach is grounded in field observation, careful analysis and respectful collaboration. We value local knowledge as much as technical expertise, and believe that useful research should be accessible beyond the research setting.

Our purpose

Evidence that
leads somewhere.

We aim to contribute knowledge that helps identify environmental priorities, strengthen local capacity and inform decisions. We are interested in the connections between healthy ecosystems, livelihoods and long-term community well-being.

Every landscape has its own story. We start by listening, learning and documenting what is happening on the ground.
