A little about us

We turn thoughtful research into meaningful action.

NZINC brings people, science and place together to understand environmental challenges and build solutions that last.

From freshwater habitats to climate-resilient agriculture, our work begins with careful observation and grows through collaboration with communities, researchers and local institutions.
