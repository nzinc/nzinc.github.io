Who We Are

Advancing Science, Conserving Biodiversity, and Building a Sustainable Future for Nepal.

Nepal Zebrafish in Nature Center (NZINC) is a non-profit, non-political, public-interest research organization dedicated to advancing scientific research, molecular and phenotypic diagnostics, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable agriculture in Nepal. Our work brings together research, innovation, and community engagement to address emerging challenges in environmental sustainability, agriculture, and food security.

Our research spans zebrafish and other fish species, indigenous and improved livestock, crop protection, and climate change. Through field-based investigations, laboratory research, policy advocacy, and agricultural extension, we work to generate scientific knowledge, support evidence-based decisions, and strengthen the connection between research and practical solutions.

We envision an inclusive, science-driven future where biodiversity is valued, agricultural systems are resilient, and scientific knowledge contributes to the well-being of communities and the sustainable management of Nepal's natural resources.

What we do

Four pillars that drive
our impact.

Zebrafish & Aquatic Biodiversity

Investigating zebrafish and other freshwater fish through ecological studies, morphometric characterization, molecular research, and habitat assessment to understand and conserve Nepal's aquatic biodiversity.

Molecular & Phenotypic Research

Advancing molecular diagnostics, genetic characterization, and phenotypic assessment to support scientific discovery, disease identification, and improved understanding of biological diversity.

Sustainable Agriculture & Livestock

Supporting crop protection, indigenous and improved livestock research, climate-resilient agricultural practices, and sustainable production systems to strengthen Nepal's food security.

Climate Change & Conservation

Studying environmental change and its effects on biodiversity and agricultural systems while promoting conservation, evidence-based policy, and sustainable management of natural resources.
