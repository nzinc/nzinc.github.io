Project 01Aquatic research

Mapping Zebrafish Streams & Morphological Characterization of Native Zebrafish in Nepal

Mapping aquatic habitats and documenting the morphological characteristics of native zebrafish (Danio rerio) across freshwater streams in Nepal.

Stream mapping & field surveys

Identifying and georeferencing micro-habitats, agricultural drainage systems and natural breeding environments across local freshwater streams.

Morphological characterization

Documenting body measurements, fin structure, stripe patterns and sexual dimorphism among native populations.

Ecological assessment

Considering water quality, temperature ranges and habitat pressures that may affect freshwater ecosystems.

Project 02Climate resilience

Municipal Collaboration on Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) in Nepal

Working with local authorities, agricultural service centres and farming communities to support climate-resilient agricultural practices.

Local government alignment

Coordinating with municipal agricultural offices and local stakeholders to connect climate-resilient practices with local development planning.

Community-led action

Supporting practical collaboration with farming communities across local agro-ecological settings.

Sustainability & policy scaling

Developing community-driven action plans and policy briefs to support wider adoption of sustainable farming practices.
